Francesco Milano is an architect and designer based in Zurich. His work focuses on digital design and fabrication technologies. He holds a PhD from ETH Zurich, where he conducted research at Gramazio Kohler Research. He previously worked as a computational designer at Knippers Helbig Advanced Engineering in Stuttgart and founded TaMaCo, an experimental design and fabrication lab in Buenos Aires.
