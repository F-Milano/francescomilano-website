Francesco Milano is an architect and designer based in Zurich, focusing on computational designand digital fabrication. He holds a PhD from ETH Zurich (Gramazio Kohler Research), where he conducted research on large-scale robotic 3D printing. Previously, he worked as a computational designer at Knippers Helbig Advanced Engineering in Stuttgart and founded TaMaCo, an experimental design and fabrication laboratory in Buenos Aires.
